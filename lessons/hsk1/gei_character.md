# 给 (gěi) — Lesson

**Date:** 2026-04-28

---

## What is 给?

**给 (gěi)** is a very common Chinese character with **two main uses**:

---

## 1️⃣ As a Verb → "to give"

> **Subject + 给 + Person + Thing**

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 我给你一本书。| Wǒ gěi nǐ yī běn shū. | I give you a book. |
| 她给我钱。| Tā gěi wǒ qián. | She gives me money. |

---

## 2️⃣ As a Preposition → "to / for"

> **Subject + 给 + Person + Verb**

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 我给你打电话。| Wǒ gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà. | I'll call you. (lit. I for you make a call) |
| 妈妈给我做饭。| Māma gěi wǒ zuò fàn. | Mom cooks for me. |

---

## 💡 Quick Tip to Remember

- If **给** is followed by a **person + noun** → it's a **verb** (to give)
- If **给** is followed by a **person + action verb** → it's a **preposition** (for/to)
