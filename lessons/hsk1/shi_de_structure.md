# 是……的 Structure (shì…de) 🇨🇳

This is a very useful grammar pattern! It is used to **emphasize specific details** about a completed action — like *when*, *where*, *how*, or *with whom* it happened. The action itself is already known; you are just highlighting one detail.

---

## 📐 Basic Formula

> **Subject + 是 + [emphasized detail] + Verb + 的**

---

## 🔍 What Can You Emphasize?

### 1. ⏰ Time — *When?*
| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我**是**昨天**来的**。 | Wǒ shì zuótiān lái de. | I came **yesterday**. |
| 他**是**上午打电话**的**。 | Tā shì shàngwǔ dǎ diànhuà de. | He called **in the morning**. |

---

### 2. 📍 Place — *Where?*
| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我**是**在中国学的汉语。 | Wǒ shì zài Zhōngguó xué de Hànyǔ. | I learned Chinese **in China**. |
| 他**是**在家吃的饭。 | Tā shì zài jiā chī de fàn. | He ate **at home**. |

---

### 3. 🚌 Method — *How?*
| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你**是**怎么来的？ | Nǐ shì zěnme lái de? | How did you get here? |
| 我**是**坐公共汽车来的。 | Wǒ shì zuò gōnggòng qìchē lái de. | I came **by bus**. |

---

### 4. 👥 With whom?
| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你**是**跟谁来的？ | Nǐ shì gēn shéi lái de? | Who did you come **with**? |
| 我**是**跟朋友来的。 | Wǒ shì gēn péngyou lái de. | I came **with a friend**. |

---

## ❗ Key Points to Remember

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| ✅ Only used for **completed** actions | 我是昨天买的。✔ |
| ✅ 是 can be **omitted** in casual speech | 我昨天买的。✔ |
| ✅ 的 goes **after the verb** (or after the object) | 我是在北京买的票。✔ |
| ❌ Don't use it for **future** actions | ~~我是明天去的。~~ ✘ |

---

## 💡 Compare These Two Sentences

| Sentence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 我**买了**书。 | I **bought** a book. (focus: the action) |
| 我**是**在图书馆买**的**书。 | I bought the book **at the library**. (focus: the place) |

---

## 🌟 Quick HSK Tip
> At **HSK2** level, focus on time and place emphasis — they appear most often in exams! Practice with simple words you know: 昨天 (zuótiān), 今天 (jīntiān), 在家 (zài jiā), 坐车 (zuò chē).

---

## ✏️ Practice Exercises

Try translating these sentences using 是……的:

1. I came **yesterday**.
2. She learned Chinese **in Shanghai**.
3. He came **by taxi**.
4. They came **with a teacher**.

> **Answers:**
> 1. 我是昨天来的。
> 2. 她是在上海学的汉语。
> 3. 他是坐出租车来的。
> 4. 他们是跟老师来的。
